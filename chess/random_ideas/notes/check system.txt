Tiles of control could be split into two variants
Tiles of movement: Can only control empty tiles
Tiles of capture: Can control empty and enemy piece tiles
We can use tiles of movement to precalculate pieces that are capable of blocking checks
And we can use tiles of capture to precalculate pieces taht are capable of capturing pieces initiating check

(*) The tiles that are affected will only require the pieces that carried influence (movement/capture) on those affected tiles will need to recalculate their influence
